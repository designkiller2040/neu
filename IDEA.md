## The problem (in the author's words)
- Thoughts come in huge numbers and are random. I believe many of them and treat them as possible reality.
- On "low days" that belief triggers a strong chemical/emotional response in the body: chest, shoulders, breath, voice.
- Negative emotions already active in the body get stuck. I try to "not feel that", so they grow or stay the same.
- If an emotion isn't released and something else happens later in the day, it hits harder. Emotions layer.

## The app
Tips and practices for:
- Coming back to momentum.
- Gratitude for being alive.
- Clearing the body:
  - shaking and jumping
  - moaning sounds or vibrations
  - singing
  - conscious movement (dance)
