# Free marketing plan (zero budget)

Reality check: a new channel usually has near-zero views for the first 1-3 months. Plan for 3-6 months of
steady posting before monetization (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours). Time and consistency are the investment.

## 1. Shorts are your free growth engine
Cat clips are made for Shorts. `--shorts` makes a vertical Short from each compilation and schedules it
~4 hours before the long video, linking to it. Shorts get far more free reach than long videos for new channels.
Aim for 1-3 Shorts/day; every Short's description and pinned comment points to the full compilation.

## 2. Make each video easy to click and finish
- Title: a clear promise + emoji ("Cats Being Cats 😸"), under 60 chars. Thumbnail: one close-up cat face, big 2-3 word text.
- Hook: first 3 seconds must be the funniest/cutest clip. Put the best clip first.
- Ask for the subscribe once, near the start ("new cats every day").
- Group videos into playlists ("Funny cats", "Kittens", "Relaxing cats") so viewers keep watching.

## 3. Cross-post your own videos (free reach)
Post the same vertical Shorts to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and Pinterest (Idea Pins/Pins link to YouTube).
Put the channel link in each bio. Pexels/Pixabay clips are licensed for this too.

## 4. Communities, without spamming
- Share in cat subreddits/groups only where self-promotion is allowed, and read each rule first. Better: be a real member, comment on others' posts, share when it's relevant.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour: the algorithm rewards early engagement.
- Comment genuinely on small cat channels' videos; don't copy-paste links.

## 5. Search (SEO)
Use phrases people search: "funny cats", "cute kittens", "cats for relaxing", "cat videos for cats". Add 3-5 tags,
a 2-line description with the keyword, and chapters in longer videos. "Cat TV" / "videos for cats to watch" (1-3 hours of birds/mice) are low-competition and long-watch-time, which helps the 4,000-hour goal.

## 6. Collaborate
Offer shout-outs to small pet/cat channels in your niche, and ask real cat owners if you may feature their clips (written permission) for a credit and a link. Permissioned clips make your channel more unique.

## 7. Track and double down
After 2 weeks check YouTube Studio: keep the topics with the best average view duration, drop the rest.

## Don'ts (they get channels banned)
Sub-for-sub swaps, view bots, buying subs, mass-posting links, spamming comments, posting clips you don't have rights to.

## Other income once you grow
Affiliate links in descriptions (pet products, Amazon Associates), merch (print-on-demand needs no upfront cost), channel memberships later.
